Motion design roots, occasional pipeline troublemaker.

I’m a creative technologist and designer with close to two decades of experience making pretty colors move. My career started in broadcast graphics and art direction, and somewhere along the way I discovered that I was just as interested in building the tools as I was in using them. So, while I was a senior designer at a major broadcast network, I quietly automated my own job. I took the network’s branding guidelines, resources and templates and coded them into an After Effects pipeline that would automatically make promos for me each week. It worked exceedingly well (so well, in fact, that I started having to ask for more work just to keep busy). That project set the direction for everything since, combining design and code, together, on purpose.

These days I split my time between a Creative Technologist role at the University of Sydney (where that combination turns out to be genuinely useful at scale) and an international freelance practice, taking on projects with studios and agencies, building MOGRT template systems, writing ExtendScript automations, and helping teams figure out how AI actually fits into a real production pipeline. I care a lot about design and direction, but I care just as much about making production efficient. If you’re looking for someone who can do both (and talk sensibly about how they connect), I’d love to hear what you’re working on.
